Bhagat Singh

A young revolutionary who became a symbol of courage and sacrifice.

Explore His Life
01

About Bhagat Singh

Bhagat Singh was an Indian revolutionary and one of the most remembered figures of India's freedom movement. He was known for his courage, strong ideas and dedication to India's independence.

He believed that young people could play an important role in changing society. His life and sacrifice continue to inspire students and citizens across India.

02

Early Life

Bhagat Singh was born on 28 September 1907 in Banga, in the Punjab region. From an early age, he was interested in India's freedom struggle and developed a strong sense of patriotism. He later became involved in revolutionary activities and writings.

03

Important Timeline

1907

Birth

Bhagat Singh was born on 28 September 1907 in Banga, Punjab region.

1926

Naujawan Bharat Sabha

He became associated with the Naujawan Bharat Sabha, an organisation of young people.

1928

Revolutionary Activities

He became increasingly active in the revolutionary movement and worked with fellow revolutionaries.

1929

Central Legislative Assembly

Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt threw non-lethal bombs in the Central Legislative Assembly as a political protest.

1931

Martyrdom

Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev were executed on 23 March 1931.

04

His Contribution

Courage

He showed remarkable courage while facing colonial authority and imprisonment.

Ideas

His writings and political ideas encouraged young people to think about freedom and social change.

Inspiration

His life became a lasting source of inspiration for generations of Indians.

“They may kill me, but they cannot kill my ideas.”

— Commonly attributed to Bhagat Singh

05

Remembering Bhagat Singh

Bhagat Singh's short life left a deep impact on India's freedom movement. His courage, sacrifice and commitment to his ideas continue to make him an important figure in Indian history.
